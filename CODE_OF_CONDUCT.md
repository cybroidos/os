# Code of conduct

CybroidOS is made by people from many places and backgrounds. We want taking
part to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their experience,
nationality, religion, gender, age, ability or appearance.

**We expect** patience and respect, especially with people who are new;
criticism of ideas and code, never of people; and owning our mistakes.

**We don't accept** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, or
anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, and its enforcement guidelines. It applies in this
organisation's repositories, issues and discussions, and wherever someone
represents the project.

**To report something,** write to **conduct@cybroid.com**. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, code or contributions, and
may block anyone, temporarily or permanently, for behaviour they judge
inappropriate.
